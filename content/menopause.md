> **Coming soon.** ThriveCare isn't offering menopause care just yet. For now, care focuses on medical weight management.

## What's planned

Once menopause care launches, the plan is to offer virtual visits that take time to listen. Care will start with a careful look at symptoms, medical history and goals, then a personal plan built around what matters most to each person.

That support may include help with common changes such as hot flashes, night sweats, sleep, mood, brain fog, energy, weight changes, joint comfort and sexual health. Treatment options can include hormone therapy when appropriate, non-hormonal approaches, and practical guidance on nutrition, movement, sleep and stress. Follow-up visits will be part of care so the plan can be adjusted over time.

All appointments will be virtual and available to adults across Ontario.

## Want to know when it's available?

If you'd like to hear when menopause care is ready, [email your interest](mailto:info@thrivecare.ca?subject=Menopause%20care%20interest) and you'll be notified.
